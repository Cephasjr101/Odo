Terms of service

1. The service

Shore connects customers with independent restaurants, pharmacies and stores. The sale contract is between you and the store; Shore provides the marketplace and payments.

2. Orders & payment

Orders are paid in advance via Paystack (MoMo or card). Prices are set by stores. The platform charges stores a commission per order (shown to store owners).

3. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before the store accepts them, and by the store or customer with a recorded reason. Refunds for cancelled orders are processed within 72 hours (3 business days).

4. Conduct

No fraudulent orders, no abuse of stores or riders. Violations lead to suspension.

5. Insurance

Shore provides order protection coverage of up to GHS 1,000,000 per incident when both parties provide sufficient evidence (photos, order records). Claims are reviewed within 72 hours (3 business days) and approved payouts paid within the same window.

Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone.
  • Orders and delivery addresses you provide.
  • Store details and item listings for vendors.

How we use it

  • To process orders and payments.
  • To notify you about order status.
  • To keep the service secure.

What we never do

We never sell your data. Payout details are never shared with other users.

Your control

Refund policy

Customers

If an order is cancelled before delivery (by you or the store), your payment is refunded in full within 72 hours (3 business days). If goods arrive damaged or wrong, contact support with your order reference and photos — most disputes resolve within 72 hours.

Stores

Your 85% share lands in your balance as soon as an order is paid. Payouts to MoMo or bank are processed within 48 hours of request.
